# Interview questions for one post

A good question points at one moment and gets a scene back. A question about a category ("your strengths",
"your biggest challenge") gets a summary back, and a summary cannot be posted. Ask one at a time, in roughly
this order, and follow the user when they get animated about something off the list.

## The sequence

1. **The moment.** "When did this last happen to you?" or "What were you doing when you noticed this?"
2. **The walk-through.** "What happened first? And then?"
3. **The detail.** "What is the number in this, and what does it measure?" or "What was the tool, the place,
   the document?"
4. **The turn.** "What did you get wrong at the time?" or "What did you believe before this that you don't
   now?"
5. **The cost.** "What did it cost you: money, time, a client, a view you liked?"
6. **The position.** "Who would disagree with your take, and what would they say?"
7. **The reader.** "What should someone reading this do differently tomorrow?"
8. **Naming.** "Can I name the company and the people, or keep them anonymous?" Ask before any name goes in.

Stop once the spine is full; 5 questions is often enough.

## Pressing once

A vague answer gets one follow-up, then accept it:

| They said | Ask once |
|---|---|
| "we improved it a lot" | "By how much, measured how?" |
| "recently", "a while back" | "Which month?" |
| "a big client" | "Can I name them, or describe them without the name?" |
| "it was hard" | "What was the hardest hour of it?" |
| "people always say" | "Who said it to you last?" |

A rough number said out loud as rough is fine; write it as rough.

## Hard moments

- **Silence**: usually recall, not refusal. Wait, then offer a concrete prompt ("What did you do on Monday?").
- **A long tangent**: let it run. The usable detail is often in the tangent.
- **"Rather not say"**: drop that line for good and offer to record it under Off limits in the persona (recorded once they confirm).

## Questions that waste the turn

"Tell me about yourself", "What makes you unique?", "What are your content pillars?", "Any other wins?",
"Describe a challenge you overcame". Each asks for a performance or a list, and gets adjectives back.
